Account Deletion & Data Request Portal

Delete within the App

The fastest way to delete your account is through the Social-Fintech app settings.

1
Open Social-Fintech and ensure you are logged in.
2
Navigate to your Profile tab in the bottom menu.
3
Tap the Settings (gear icon) in the top right corner.
4
Select Security & Privacy, then tap Delete Account.
5
Verify your identity via password or SMS code and confirm the final request.

Data Handling Policy

Immediate Removal

Upon request, your personal profile (name, email, avatar), authentication tokens, and all active device sessions are revoked and marked for removal immediately.

30-Day Grace Period

Activity logs, transaction history, and uploaded media files are moved to a restricted storage state for 30 days before permanent purging to allow for accidental deletion recovery.

Legal Retention: Please note that certain data points (such as financial transaction records) may be retained beyond the 30-day window if required by applicable laws for tax, audit, or regulatory compliance purposes.

Manual Request

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot access your account, you can request deletion via our support team.
