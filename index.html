Ledger-Based Transaction Security

All Silver & Gold Seal currency transfers are guarded by an immutable, server-controlled double-entry bookkeeping ledger system. We enforce strict database-level ACID transactions to ensure zero inflation, prevent double-spending, and isolate virtual transfers.
