3. Virtual Economy (Seals) Rules

BrightBund utilizes a double-entry bookkeeping ledger to verify and log all virtual currency transactions (Silver Seals & Gold Seals). By participating in the economy, you acknowledge:

  • No Real-World Value: Seals are solely engagement metrics. They have no real cash equivalent, cannot be converted to fiat currency, and are not redeemable outside of BrightBund.
  • Server Consensus as Truth: Our server-side double-entry ledger database is the only legal source of truth. Any client-side modifications, visual glitches, or cached values showing incorrect balances are invalid.
  • Idempotency Enforcement: Double-spending requests or ledger tampering will cause immediate transaction failure and possible account suspension.

4. Reputation Tiers & Anti-Abuse

Progression tiers ( Quartz, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Sovereign) are calculated asynchronously by our backend workers to prevent spamming and secure the platform. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for graph manipulation:

  • Anti-Farming: We track transaction sequences. Circular farming (e.g. User A transferring seals to User B, and User B returning them to User A) is strictly prohibited.
  • Rate Limits: API calls to spend or send Seals are heavily throttled to block bot nodes.

Account Enforcement

Accounts identified as bot nets, transaction farms, or coordinate spoofers will suffer immediate forfeiture of all accumulated Silver & Gold Seals, along with permanent progression rank ban.
